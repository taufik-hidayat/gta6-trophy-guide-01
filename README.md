## The list at a glance

55 trophies on PlayStation 5 — 1 platinum, 2 gold, 6 silver, 46 bronze — 28 of them hidden on PlayStation until earned. All 55 are single-player: there are no online trophies. PowerPyx rates the platinum 4/10 at about 200 hours, in a single playthrough.

## What the guide gives you

- **Tracker** — a checklist of all 55 trophies with official names, descriptions and grades, saved in your browser (localStorage) with export to a file and import back. Filter by grade, by kind (story, missable, collectibles, activities, online), or by search; sort by guide order, grade, or name.
- **Spoiler setting** — with "Hide spoilers" on, the names, descriptions and guides of hidden story trophies stay masked until you earn them.
- **Platinum roadmap** — four stages to Vice Got a Price!: play the five chapters (60–80 hours), finish the strangers and side-character strands (~10 hours), work the 100% Completion checklist (~100 hours), then money, crime and the last one-offs. Every stage lists the trophies it earns.
- **Missable flags** — only one trophy is flagged as possibly missable: Positive Mental Attitude. Its first meditation spot is offered at Jason's house in Chapter 1 and is gone after that chapter, so the guide tells you to do it before Chapter 1 ends. Everything else can be earned in free roam after the story.
- **Cited guides** — every trophy's guide is written in our own words and cites its sources beside it (Exophase and PSNProfiles for the official list and progress targets, PowerPyx for the roadmap and missable call, GTA Zone for the per-trophy unlock tips). Where the guide sites have not pinned a trigger down yet — In The Beginning…, Capital Offense, Bad Luck, I Guess, The Truth Is Out There, Ten Pin — the guide says so plainly instead of guessing.
- **Fully offline** — static files under `app/`, no build step, no network request at run time. Open `app/index.html` from any static server with the network off and it works.

## The road to the platinum, in short

1. **Play the story (Chapters 1–5), 60–80 hours.** All 12 story trophies, plus the Chapter 1 meditation. Scan vehicles and study animals as you travel — the checklist later wants 200 of 277 base-model vehicles and 100 of 255 animals.
2. **Strangers and side characters, ~10 hours.** The 8 Strangers & Freaks strands that count, the final missions for Andy, Kenton, Billy, Randy and Tyrone, and Bret's hunt.
3. **The 100% Completion checklist, ~100 hours.** 70 of the 80 challenges, 42 Shooting Range golds, 250 stunts, 14 drone-race locations, 20 races, 3 card sets, 100 WAiNK spots, 15 of 18 meditation spots. A Long Road, the gold for 100% completion, is the hardest trophy — and the last one before the platinum.
4. **Money, crime and cleanup.** 5 of 8 bank vaults, an armored truck, a jewelry store unseen, one illegal operation per gang, $250,000 laundered, $100,000 spent, all 8 garages, 10 vehicles on Scooter Bros — then the platinum pops.

Two rules for the whole run: there is no mission replay after the story, so story trophies are earned by playing the missions when they appear; and cheats disable trophies and saving, so keep trophy runs cheat-free.
